Nidaan

One health record. Three ways in.

Patients own their records and decide what to share. Clinics manage the front desk. Doctors see only what is assigned to them.

  • Patient · keep reports, answer intake questions, share an episode with a clinic for a set time.
  • Clinic · register arrivals, assign doctors, send intake requests, accept shares.
  • Doctor · see assigned patients, their intake summary, reports read by OCR and red flags.
